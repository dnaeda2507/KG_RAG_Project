  1. INTRODUCTION
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  This report documents Phase 1 (Dataset Exploration) and Phase 2 (Domain
  Selection) of the KG-Infused RAG project.

  The Wikidata5M knowledge graph contains approximately 4.8 million entities
  and 20.6 million triples stored across the following files:

    wikidata5m_raw_data/wikidata5m_all_triplet.txt   — all subject|rel|object triples
    wikidata5m_raw_data/wikidata5m_alias/wikidata5m_entity.txt — entity aliases
    wikidata5m_raw_data/wikidata5m_alias/wikidata5m_relation.txt — relation aliases
    wikidata5m_raw_data/wikidata5m_text.txt — full entity text descriptions

  The goal of Phase 1 is to identify Türkiye-related entities, city
  distributions, and relevant relation types. Phase 2 selects the most
  suitable domain for creating multi-hop questions.

  Relation usage in Türkiye context:

   COUNTRY (14,114)
     — Most frequent: used for citizenship, company country, entity location.
     — Powers 3-hop questions: Film → Director → BirthCity → COUNTRY → Türkiye

   COUNTRY_OF_CITIZENSHIP (6,250)
     — Person ↔ Türkiye links; essential for cast-member nationality questions.
     — Example: "muhteşem yüzyıl" CAST_MEMBER Deniz Cakir
                → COUNTRY_OF_CITIZENSHIP → turkiye

   COUNTRY_OF_ORIGIN (759)
     — Film and product origin; key relation for cinema domain.
     — All 759 Turkish films identified via this relation.

   PLACE_OF_BIRTH (101)
     — Directly answers "where was X born?" questions.
     — Core relation for 2-hop cinema/football questions.

   HEADQUARTERS_LOCATION (17)
     — Company and institution HQ in Turkey.
     — Used in companies domain (707 entities).

   FILMING_LOCATION (63)
     — Turkish locations used in film shoots.
     — Present in cinema subgraph but not used in question templates.

   Note: MEMBER_OF_SPORTS_TEAM and DIRECTOR are not direct Q43 relations;
   they are 1-hop relations used in multi-hop paths through Turkish entities.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  4.4  Domain Selection Decision
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  PRIMARY Domain   : CINEMA (Türk Sineması)
  SECONDARY Domain : FOOTBALL (Türk Futbolu)

  Justification for Cinema (Primary):
    759 Türk filmi tespit edildi. DIRECTOR, CAST_MEMBER, AWARD_RECEIVED,
    EDUCATED_AT, GENRE, COUNTRY_OF_ORIGIN relation'ları ile 619 adet 2-hop
    ve 557 adet 3-hop path oluşturulabilmektedir. 26 farklı relation türü ile
    en çeşitli domain konumundadır. Tüm 3.4 kriterleri karşılanmıştır.

  Justification for Football (Secondary):
    11 futbol kulübü ve 699 futbolcu tespit edildi. 575 adet 2-hop ve 555
    adet 3-hop path mevcuttur. Avg-relations kriteri tam sağlanmasa da zengin
    path yapısı nedeniyle ikincil domain olarak seçilmiştir.

  Multi-Hop Path Templates (Cinema):
    2-hop: Film → DIRECTOR         → PLACE_OF_BIRTH   → City
    2-hop: Film → CAST_MEMBER      → PLACE_OF_BIRTH   → City
    2-hop: Film → DIRECTOR         → AWARD_RECEIVED   → Award
    2-hop: Film → CAST_MEMBER      → COUNTRY_OF_CITIZENSHIP → Country
    2-hop: Film → DIRECTOR         → EDUCATED_AT      → Institution
    3-hop: Film → DIRECTOR         → PLACE_OF_BIRTH   → COUNTRY → Country
    3-hop: Film → CAST_MEMBER      → PLACE_OF_BIRTH   → COUNTRY → Country
    3-hop: Film → DIRECTOR         → EDUCATED_AT      → COUNTRY → Country

  5. LIMITATIONS  (Section 10.2 & 10.3)
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  1. Historical place name aliases
     Ankara stored as 'Angora, Turkey', İzmir as 'ismir, turkey'. These
     do not match standard English or Turkish spellings, creating entity-
     linking difficulties in downstream phases.

  2. Turkish character encoding gaps
     Characters ş, ğ, ü, ö, ı, ç lack consistent aliases in
     wikidata5m_entity.txt, causing entity-linking failures in Phase 4
     (e.g., 'halit refiğ' not found via alias matching).

  3. wikidata5m_text.txt not used as retrieval corpus
     The text file contains richer Wikipedia-style descriptions. Using it
     as a retrieval corpus in Phase 4 is noted as a key improvement.

  4. Company domain sparse
     707 company entities exist but Neo4j traversal returned 0 related
     entities — the company subgraph has very few traversable outbound edges.

  5. Music domain insufficient
     Only 12 music artists — far below the 50-question requirement.
